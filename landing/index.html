Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about implementation and compliance.

What hardware is required for the checkout counter?
Any standard Windows PC or POS terminal with a USB or Bluetooth 1D/2D barcode scanner, 80mm ESC/POS thermal printer, and standard RJ11 cash drawer.
How does offline synchronization protect against power outages?
Sales are committed immediately to the local transactional SQLite engine. When power or internet recovers, our outbox sync daemon pushes records with idempotent keys to prevent double-charging.
Can custom roles be created for cashiers vs auditors?
Yes. Granular RBAC enforces that Cashiers can only perform checkouts, Branch Managers can approve returns, and Accountants/Auditors have access to financial ledgers.
